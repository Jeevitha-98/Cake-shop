— The Chronology —

Our Evolution Through
Confectionery Chronology.

2016 The Inception // Paris

Classic Apprenticeship Foundations

Starting as a micro private baking studio under a classical apprentice wing in Paris, mastering ancestral European sponge folding techniques and mirror lacquer metrics.

2021 The Shift // Geometric

Architectural Sugar Modeling

Pivoting standard pastry production boundaries to introduce structural engineering blueprints, calculating stress variances on tall multi-layered cake frames.

2026 The Salon // Present

The High-Fashion Cake Salon

Launching our flagship digital concierge studio showroom, custom-building bespoke sugar sculptures using clean organic wildflower reductions for life's prime events.
