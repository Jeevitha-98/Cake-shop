— Concierge Inquiries —

Atelier Guidance &
Bespoke Protocol.

How far in advance must I lock my commission blueprint?

Because we limit production weekly to guarantee total structural focus and material excellence, signature tiered wedding artworks require booking reservations at least 4 to 6 weeks before your celebration season calendar lines open.

Can the design matrix handle out-of-state transportation stress factors?

Yes. Every multi-tiered custom sculpture is hand-drafted using an internal system of support anchors and dowel balancing wires. This design minimizes internal shear stress, allowing secure transport inside temperature-controlled vans.

Do you conduct private flavor profile tasting evaluations?

Absolutely. Validating a blueprint booking questionnaire unlocks an exclusive invitation to our private tasting salon. Here, you can sample single-origin cocoa profiles, fresh fruit coulis reductions, and wildflower wildflower essences.
